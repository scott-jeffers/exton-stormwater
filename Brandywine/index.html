About this map

Built from five linked HEC-RAS 7 rain-on-grid models covering 271 sq mi of the Brandywine above Chadds Ford: 1-meter USGS lidar, county land cover and soils, 1,339 road crossings and five flood-control dams (Barneston, Marsh Creek, Wharton, Beaver Creek, Hibernia). Upstream outflows feed the downstream models.

Coverage: the 10-year 6-hr storm covers all five models. The 24-hr design storms cover the upper watershed for now (Upper East Branch, Buck/Doe Run), and the Ida replay adds Downingtown. Coatesville and the main stem are being added. Areas a scenario does not cover show no flooding, which does not mean they stay dry.

Limits: storm sewers are not modeled, and many culvert sizes and dam outlets were estimated, not surveyed. Calibration against USGS gauges is under way: the 100-year matches the Dowlin Forge gauge record within about 6%, but smaller storms and Ida run low. Flood maps show likely problem areas. They do not predict exact depths. Always confirm conditions in the field.

Live data: NWS (api.weather.gov, WPC QPF), NOAA MRMS and NEXRAD via Iowa Environmental Mesonet, USGS Water Data, NOAA NWPS, FEMA NFHL.
