Results by model

Peak flow vs Flood Study reference flows

Model 10-yr peaks run about 20–30% under (main stem: 12,895 vs 17,137 cfs, −25%) the Flood Study's 10-yr flows. That is expected for now: the Study's flows are USGS frequency flows, which match a 24-hour design storm, and these runs used a 6-hour storm. The 24-hour runs (plans p12–p17 are already built) are the like-for-like check.

24-hour design storms vs Flood Study (like-for-like)

First 24-hour results. Buck/Doe Run's 10-yr is within about 20% of the Study (the Study's figure adds two tributaries). Upper East Branch's 100-yr is 67% above the FEMA 100-yr at Dowlin Forge. That gap is too large to accept: the next check is the soil/infiltration settings and whether the 24-hr SCS Type II pattern is too peaky for these watersheds.

Ida high-water marks, East Branch (Downingtown)

The modeled Ida water surface is 3.5–4.3 ft below every surveyed mark, and lands almost exactly on the FEMA 100-yr elevation. A consistent offset like this points at the rain input, not the hydraulics: this run spread a uniform 6-hour total over each model, and Ida's heaviest bands (7.0 in at Modena, 6.2 in at Chester Springs) fell in about 3 hours. Radar rainfall (MRMS) is the fix. West Branch and main-stem marks get checked once Coatesville Ida and the main-stem runs finish.

Ida rainfall: radar vs what the first Ida runs used

NOAA MRMS radar (gauge-corrected, hourly, ~1 km) shows Downingtown's area got about 7.1 in, 20% more than the 5.9 in spread evenly in the first run, and over about 8 hours rather than 6. Turn on the radar layer (top-left layer control) to see the pattern. Ida reruns with this hour-by-hour radar grid are underway.

What these runs are

  • Five linked HEC-RAS 7.0.1 2D models; rain falls on every cell (rain-on-grid), with lidar terrain, Chester County land cover and SSURGO soils.
  • Upstream outflows feed the next model: Upper East Branch + Exton → Downingtown; Buck/Doe Run → Coatesville; both → main stem.
  • Dams are modeled as embankments with their spillways: Barneston, Marsh Creek, Wharton, Beaver Creek, Hibernia. Struble and Rock Run are in the terrain.
  • 10-yr storm: 6-hr SCS Type II, Atlas 14 depth.
  • Ida 2021: 6-hr Type II pattern scaled to the Flood Study's gauge totals for each model (4.3–6.4 in). Uniform rain; not radar.
  • Depth maps show max depth ≥ 0.5 ft on a 20-ft grid. Small isolated puddles are removed.
  • Buildings count = county footprints touched by ≥ 0.5 ft of water. It is a screening count, not a first-floor damage count.
